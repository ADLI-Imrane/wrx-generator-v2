# WRX Product Roadmap

## Product direction

WRX is an integrated digital toolbox for individuals, freelancers, startups, and companies. Its differentiation should come from connected assets and workflows—not the number of unrelated generators. Keep useful standalone entry points, but make it easy for work to flow between WRX tools.

**Product principle:** Create → Share → Secure → Track.

Examples of connected workflows:

- Profile / brand identity → business card → digital card → email signature or WRX Page.
- Destination → dynamic QR or smart link → protected or expiring share → real analytics.
- Campaign assets → consistent identity → centralized team management.

The current links, QR codes, click/scan analytics, and local password generator remain part of the product. The roadmap extends their connections; it does not replace them.

## Ordered evolution

1. **Profile / Brand Identity — COMPLETED.** Establish reusable identity details that can carry consistently into future WRX assets.
2. **Business Card Generator — NEXT.**
3. Digital Business Card.
4. Email Signature Generator.
5. Dynamic QR / Smart Links.
6. WRX Page.
7. Campaigns.
8. Team Workspace / Brand Kit.

Move one phase at a time; do not begin a later phase by implication. Each phase should reuse or connect existing WRX assets where useful.

## Product and value guardrails

- Free should remain genuinely useful. Pro value should grow through control, dynamic assets, customization, and analytics. Business value can grow through scale, campaigns, teams, branding, and centralized management.
- Preserve current API-defined plan names, capabilities, and billing behavior; future positioning does not authorize changing entitlements or promising unbuilt features.
- Build an original WRX ecosystem—not a Canva, Linktree, Bitly, or Smallpdf clone. Avoid disconnected feature accumulation.
- Introduce AI only when it provides concrete workflow value. Secure file, image, and PDF utilities are later possibilities, not near-term priorities.
- Implement no roadmap phase until it is explicitly selected. This document records direction; it does not authorize feature work.
